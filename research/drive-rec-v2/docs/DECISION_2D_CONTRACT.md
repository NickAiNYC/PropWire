## 2. Market, source, selection, quote age, entry prices, benchmarks

| Requirement | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Market | NFL player receiving yards, over/under on the **primary** line initially (alternates excluded) |
| Source | Licensed, timestamped sportsbook prices with per-quote bookmaker timestamps; **synthetic quotes forbidden** in the pilot (any synthetic price makes the run HYPOTHETICAL and non-promotable) |
| Selection | Strictly point-in-time; predictions frozen **pregame**; decision timestamps **T−60m primary** (roster state at decision time is a legal universe input), sensitivity **T−180m** and **T−15m**; closing benchmark = last snapshot ≤ kickoff |
| Quote age | ≤60s primary live-analysis tier with 120s/300s sensitivity. **Recorded limitation:** 5-minute historical snapshots support only the 300s tier historically; 60s/120s executability requires Phase B live capture |
| Entry prices | Actual available odds at the decision snapshot, **same book**; store decimal + American; compute in decimal |
| Benchmarks | Same-book contemporaneous quote (primary); cross-book consensus median and same-book closing reported separately |

## 3. Settlement

- Actual receiving yards from nflverse play-by-play (CC BY 4.0): sum of play
  yards on completed passes to the player; official box score prevails when
  licensable; any ≥5-yard discrepancy is logged.
- **Pushes** (settled yards exactly equal the integer line): stake returned,
  realized return 0, included in ROI at 0, excluded from alert precision.
- **Voids** (market voided/player inactive): excluded from ROI and precision,
  always recorded in the availability ledger.

## 4. Metrics and uncertainty

Realized ROI, expected ROI (`P̂·D−1`), CLV (`p_close_novig·D_entry−1`, vig
removed by two-sided normalization), alert precision (settled wins / settled),
**selection-conditional calibration** (mean `P̂(over)` vs realized over-rate
*inside the selected subset* — the Decision 2C review's requirement), and
coverage (game-level, player-level, quote-age distribution). Every headline
number with a 95% **game-clustered bootstrap CI** (1000 replicates, fixed seeds).

Alert thresholds: primary **EV ≥ 5%** (the band where criteria v3 C4 failed);
EV ≥ 3% and ≥ 8% reported only.

## 5. Leakage protection

No feature, line, or roster information from after the decision timestamp may
enter predictions, the universe, or the transformation (enforced by assertion).
The posted line selects *which* prediction is evaluated; it is never a model
feature. Universe filtering uses only inactives published before the decision
timestamp.

## 6. Availability and missingness (explicit contract requirement)

Preserve quote availability and missingness. **A backtest must not treat the
existence of a historical quote as proof it was executable for the user at that
moment.** The ledger records per (game, book, market, snapshot): presence,
bookmaker timestamp, line, price; per (game, player): which books priced, line
values, voided; per decision timestamp: books quoting, quote-age distribution,
snapshot gaps. Coverage and missingness are reported next to every economic
number.

## 7. Precommitted gates (fail-closed; changing any after purchase voidates the pilot)

| Gate | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| E1 sample adequacy | ≥100 settled alerts from ≥25 games per primary cell, else LOW_SAMPLE → cannot support promotion |
| E2 selection-conditional calibration | gap ≤ **3.0pp** (unchanged C4 threshold) inside each primary alert threshold; clustered 95% CI within ±5.0pp |
| E3 realized ROI | mean > 0 AND clustered 95% CI lower bound ≥ −1.0% |
| E4 CLV | mean ≥ 0 |
| E5 expected-vs-realized | clustered 95% CI of (realized − expected) ROI within ±3.0pp |
| E6 data integrity & coverage | written license covering storage + commercial display of derived data; timestamps verified (bookmaker `last_update` ≤ snapshot, snapshots monotonic); ≥70% game coverage at the primary decision timestamp; complete push/void accounting |

**Promotion:** any FAIL or LOW_SAMPLE ⇒ RESEARCH_ONLY continues. Production
eligibility requires all gates PASS on the primary arm **and** human approval
via signed HUMAN_REVIEW.md **and** Phase B prospective validation. Live
sportsbook adapters remain disabled throughout Phase A.

## 8. Pilot scope and Phase B

- Phase A (historical): 2025 season + 2026 weeks 1–7 (~392 games), primary
  lines, 4 snapshots per game; vendor and books pending the verification
  checklist in `HISTORICAL_QUOTE_VENDOR_EVALUATION.md`. 2025 numbers are on a
  previously inspected holdout and are labeled as such.
- Phase B (prospective, before any production alert): precommitted live-capture
  study (observability-only, read-only adapters) at the 60s quote-age tier on
  genuinely forward data; same fail-closed rule with human approval.
- The zero-fresh-quote-alert ambiguity (correct rejection vs. over-conservatism
  vs. insufficient sensitivity) is an explicit open question the pilot is
  designed to distinguish.
